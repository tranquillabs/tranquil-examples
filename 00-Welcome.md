# Welcome to Tranquil 👋

Tranquil is three tools in one window: a **code editor**, a full **web browser**, and an
**automations runner** that lets a script drive the browser. This short, hands-on tour
shows you how they fit together — by doing, not just reading.

## How this guide works

Work through the numbered lessons in order. Each takes a couple of minutes and ends with a
link to the next one. Open a lesson two ways: click it in the tree on the **left**, or
**Cmd-click** the link right here in the text.

> 💡 **Cmd-click** any link in these docs to follow it. (In a rendered Markdown preview, a
> plain click works.)

1. **[01 · Windows & Layout](./01-Windows-and-Layout.md)** — docks, panes, tabs, and windows
2. **[02 · Browser & Tabs](./02-Browser-and-Tabs.md)** — the built-in browser
3. **[03 · Run Automations](./03-Run-Automations.md)** — run the example automations
4. **[04 · Your First Automation](./04-Your-First-Automation.md)** — write and save your own

By the end of lesson 4 you'll have **written and saved your own automation** that runs from
the command palette.
